Transportation Choke Points, Trade, and Logistic Distance JMP

2026

Abstract

This paper constructs a novel transportation-network-informed distance variable that incorporates realistic city-to-city routing into the Mayer and Zignago (2011) population-weighted distance formula, rendering bilateral distances responsive to infrastructure shocks. Using the 2023 Red Sea crisis as a natural experiment, I estimate a saturated gravity model in an event-study framework with importer–exporter–time and country-pair fixed effects on monthly bilateral trade data. Treated country pairs are those whose network-informed distance rises meaningfully following the closure of the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait, identified against a control group of unaffected pairs.

Automotive Tariffs, Spillover Effects, and the Used Car Market

2025–2026

Abstract

This paper constructs a dynamically web-scraped dataset of approximately nine million used vehicle listings in the United States, spanning April 1 through October 30, 2025, to study how automotive import tariffs propagate into the secondary vehicle market. The sample window encompasses both the Liberation Day tariff announcement and the implementation of the subsequent executive order on automotive tariffs, enabling an event-study design with two distinct treatment dates. I compare price evolution across two dimensions: vehicles by country of manufacture—domestically produced versus imported used vehicles—and vehicles by brand origin—foreign-branded versus domestically branded—to assess whether tariff exposure and consumer perception generate differential price dynamics in the used car market.

Time-Varying Distance and Trade

2025–2026

Abstract

This paper extends the Mayer and Zignago (2011) population-weighted distance formula to allow bilateral distances to fluctuate over time, using annual city-level population data from the mid-twentieth century onward. Within a gravity framework, I analyze how long-run shifts in the spatial distribution of population have shaped bilateral trade patterns over the past several decades.

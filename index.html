Job Market Paper

Transportation Choke Points, Trade, and Logistic Distance JMP

Job Market Paper, 2026

Abstract

This paper constructs a novel transportation-network-informed distance variable that incorporates realistic city-to-city routing into the Mayer and Zignago (2011) population-weighted distance formula, rendering bilateral distances responsive to infrastructure shocks. Using the 2023 Red Sea crisis as a natural experiment, I estimate a saturated gravity model in an event-study framework with importer–exporter–time and country-pair fixed effects on monthly bilateral trade data. Treated country pairs are those whose network-informed distance rises meaningfully following the closure of the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait, identified against a control group of unaffected pairs.
